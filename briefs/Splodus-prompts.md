# Brief: List of prompts for a guessing game named Splodus

Splodus is a Party Game on Smartphone where everyone plays on the same device. Each turn, a player draws a card, and has to make the others guess something as fast as possible before a bomb goes off. The way the player has to make the others guess varies. These 'mechanics' are grouped by categories in the list below. For each mechanic, come up with 5 cards (A card has a Title (its name), the name of the mechanic it uses, and a Prompt  describing the thing to make the others guess, and the way it has to be done.)

Here are the content categories and the specific mechanics we will be generating prompts for:

1. **Baseline Categories**

    **Standard Mime:** Traditional charades. No words, no sounds. (e.g., Jobs, Animals, Sports).
    **Everyday Situations:** Miming specific, relatable actions or struggles. (e.g., "Opening a stuck jar", "Walking an overly excited dog").

2. **Audio & Vocal Constraints**

    **Soundtrack Only:** The player must make the group guess a movie, scene, or situation using only sound effects and background noises. No words, no humming melodies.
    **Sing It Out:** The player cannot speak; they can only sing their description of the prompt. 
    **Lip Sync Battle:** The player must mouth the words of a famous quote, song lyric, or movie line with exaggerated, dramatic facial expressions. No sound comes out.

3. **Logic & Perspective Constraints**

    **First Person Object:** The player must act and speak as if they are the object itself. They cannot say "It is used for...". They must say "I am made of... I feel...".
    **Explain to an Alien:** The player must describe a mundane, everyday object to someone who has never been to Earth, but is forbidden from mentioning its primary function.
    **Describe What It’s Not:** The player must describe the prompt, but is forbidden from saying what it IS. They can only describe what it is NOT, what it doesn't do, or what it lacks.
   ** The Bad Translator:** The player must describe the prompt speaking as if using a broken, literal translation from another language (weird grammar, literal idioms).

4. **Physical & Spatial Constraints**

    **One Part Only:** The player must mime the prompt, but is only allowed to use one specific body part (e.g., only elbows, only facial expressions, or only feet).
    **Human Clay:** The player cannot use their own body to mime. They must physically grab the other players and arrange their arms, legs, and heads to form the shape of the prompt or a scene.
    **Blind Back-Drawing:** The player must trace the shape of the prompt with their finger on the back of another player. Crucial Rule: The player feeling the drawing must vocally describe what they feel, and everyone (including the feeler, which is mandatory for 2-player mode) can try to guess based on that description and the drawer's facial expressions.

5. **Interactive & Emotional Constraints**

    **Constrained Interrogation:** The actor has to make the audience guess something. The guessers can ask Yes/No questions, but the actor is bound by a strict, escalating emotional rule dictated by the card. 
        Example A: "Act more and more deeply vexed with every 'No' you give."
        Example B: "Act more and more exhausted and drained with every 'No' you give."
        Example C: "Act more and more hopelessly in love with every 'Yes' you give."
    **The Method Actor:** The player must describe or mime a completely mundane prompt, but is forced to perform it with an intense, mismatched emotion or persona. (e.g., "Subject: Doing the dishes. Constraint: With devouring romantic passion").
    **The Infomercial:** The player must describe the prompt as a sleazy, over-the-top TV salesman trying to aggressively sell its "features" to the group.

6. **Chaos & Meta Constraints**

    **Taboo Classic:** The card shows the main word to guess, but also lists 3 "forbidden words" underneath. If the actor says one of the forbidden words, the guessers tap a penalty button.
    **The Liar:** The actor must give the group exactly 3 clues about the prompt. Two clues must be 100% true. One clue must be a complete, blatant lie. The group has to guess the prompt and point out which clue was the lie.

## Definition of Done
1. For each card, the prompt has to fit the described mechanic ( **Standard Mime:** Traditional charades. No words, no sounds. )
2. For each mechanic, we need 5 cards
3. A card has a Title (its name), the name of the mechanic it uses, and a Prompt  describing the thing to make the others guess, and the way it has to be done.
4. Each card's prompt includes specific, clear, comedic, relatable or chaotic element that facilitate engaging gameplay, avoiding generic, dry or overly broad or literal concepts.
